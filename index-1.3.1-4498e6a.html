    Privacy

    Runes only uses account information to keep your puzzle progress connected to you.

    • If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account identifier and verified email address.
    • We store that account information with your puzzle progress, stats, and basic play activity so your history can follow you across devices.
    • We also collect technical loading details, such as app version, page path, browser, and asset timing, to keep the game running well.
    • We do not sell your email, Google account data, or puzzle history.
    • Service providers that host or operate the game may process this data for us.
    • You can play without signing in. To ask for account deletion or data questions, email dailyrunesgame@gmail.com.
